Subject: Stewardship Atlas — April–May 2026 updates

Hi Stewardship Atlas fan and/or user!

Here's a look at what's new in your atlas over the past two months.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Comments on Map Features
------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can now have conversation threads on any map feature directly from the webmap.
Specify a layer this makes sense for: field trip photos, roads being worked on,
 watertank locations under debate, whatever. Just look for the "speech bubble"
 icon to see where conversations have already started!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
QR Code
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The webmap now includes a QR code you can scan with a phone to pull up
the atlas on the spot — useful for printing or sharing at a briefing.
Click the QR button in the map header to show it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Photo Upload in the Edit Interface
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The edit map now has a direct photo upload button for point layers. Tap
or click the button, choose a geotagged photo from your device, and it
is added to the map at the photo's GPS location — the same result as
emailing a photo, but without leaving the browser.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
General Improvements
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Share link: Atlas "share" links now include everything about the map you're sharing: 
zoom level, position, and which layers are visible. This allows much more specific
communication. There is also a new "Share" button (right above the "share format" dropdown in the nav box)
which shares the exact view but without a specific location/pin. 

3D view overlays: The 3D terrain view now shows your map layers — roads,
creeks, and others — draped over the terrain surface. 

Email photo info: 
It's easier and more powerful to add data to the map when you email a geotragged photo.
* The subject line becomes the new Label on the map.
* Any layer mentioned in the first line will get the photo data added to it.
* Any further data will be stored - and any key/value pairs for existing layer Properties will be parsed out.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
