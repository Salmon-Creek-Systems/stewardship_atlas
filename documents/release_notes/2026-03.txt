Subject: Stewardship Atlas — March 2026 updates

Hi [DEPARTMENT NAME] team,

Here's a quick look at what's new in your atlas over the past month.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Email Photo Submission
------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can now add a point to the map by emailing a geotagged photo directly
to the atlas. Take a photo on your phone, attach it to an email with a
brief subject line describing what you found, and it appears on the map
within a minute or two — no login required.

Learn more: https://fireatlas.org/local/documents/help/email_photo_submission.html

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3D Terrain View
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A new 3D view is available from the interactive webmap. Click the "3D"
button in the map navigation bar to open an interactive terrain model of
your coverage area — tilt, rotate, and zoom to explore the landscape in
three dimensions.

Learn more: https://fireatlas.org/local/documents/help/3d_terrain_view.html

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Editing Tools Expanded
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The "annotate" link in the Admin Console is now labeled "edit" — and it
does more than it used to. In addition to updating feature properties, the
edit interface now lets you delete features: draw a polygon around what you
want to remove, click Delete, and confirm. No more exporting a spreadsheet
just to remove a single point.

Learn more: https://fireatlas.org/local/documents/help/editing_layer_data.html

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Console Refresh
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Admin Console has a new look — updated fonts, a cleaner layout, and
color-coded action buttons (blue for edits, red for destructive actions).
Map labels on dense layers are now deduplicated so repeated text doesn't
crowd the view. Nothing about how the console works has changed, just
easier to read and navigate.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Gazetteer Improvements
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The gazetteer PDF map book has been updated with projected grid cells,
neighbor page numbers printed on each edge so you can follow features
across pages, and a new grid index overview page as a table of contents.

Learn more: https://fireatlas.org/local/documents/help/gazetteer.html

------------------------------------------------------------------------
