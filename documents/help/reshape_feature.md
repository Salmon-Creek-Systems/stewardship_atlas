# Reshape a Feature
<!-- audience: admin -->

Use **Reshape** to fix the shape or position of a feature that's already on the
map — move a road bend, redraw an area's edge, nudge a misplaced point — without
deleting it and drawing it again. Its properties stay exactly as they were.

1. In the Admin Console, find the layer and open **Alter → Reshape**.
2. Click the feature you want to change.
3. Adjust it:
   - **Drag a corner (vertex)** to move it.
   - **Drag the small handle** halfway along an edge to add a new corner.
   - **Right-click a corner** to remove it.
   - **Drag inside the feature** (or drag a point) to move the whole thing.
4. Click **Save** to keep the new shape, or **Cancel** to discard it.

Some layers keep their areas a fixed shape — runbook regions, for example, stay
square. On those, the shape is squared up again after you save.
