# Go to a Location
<!-- audience: user -->

Jump the web map to a spot someone sent you.

1. Click **Go to…** in the panel at the top right of the web map.
2. Paste the location into the box.
3. Click **Go to** (or press Enter).

The map centres on that spot and marks it with a pin.

## What you can paste

- **Decimal coordinates:** `40.2383, -123.9608`
- **Degrees, minutes, seconds:** `40°14′18″ N 123°57′39″ W`
- **A Google Maps link**, including short `goo.gl` links
- **JSON coordinates:** `{"latitude": 40.2383, "longitude": -123.9608}`

A link someone shared from this atlas (see [Share a View or a Pin](share_location.md))
doesn't need this — just open it.
