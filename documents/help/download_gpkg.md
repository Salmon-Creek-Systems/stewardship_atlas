# Download the Atlas as a GeoPackage
<!-- audience: user -->

A GeoPackage is a single file holding every layer of the atlas, ready to open in
QGIS, ArcGIS and most other mapping software.

1. Open the atlas console.
2. In the **Downloads** section, click the GeoPackage entry (often called
   `gpkg`).
3. Save the file. It's named after the atlas and the date it was built, e.g.
   `scvfd_2026-10-06.gpkg`.

Depending on how your atlas is shared, some downloads are only offered to
atlas administrators. To download a single layer instead, see
[Export a Layer](export_layer.md).
