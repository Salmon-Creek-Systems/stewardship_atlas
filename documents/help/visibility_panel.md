# Choose What the Map Shows
<!-- audience: user -->

The **Visibility** panel at the bottom left of the web map controls everything
you see.

## Basemap

The dropdown at the top picks the background:

- **USGS Topo** — contour lines and place names
- **Satellite** — aerial imagery
- **OpenMapTiles Terrain** and **USGS Shaded Relief** — landform views
- **LIDAR Hillshade** — high-resolution terrain, on atlases that have it
- **None** — just the atlas's own layers on a blank background

## Layers

Tick or untick a layer to show or hide it. **Only rendered** shortens the list
to layers that actually have something visible in the current view.

Some layers only appear once you zoom in far enough.

## Folding the panel away

Click the **▾** at the top of the panel to fold it down to a small **▸** button
and get more map; click **▸** to bring it back. The right-hand panel folds away
the same way.

These settings only affect the live web map — not printed or PDF maps. Your
current basemap and layers are included when you
[share a view](share_location.md).
